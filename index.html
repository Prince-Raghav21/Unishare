Student Resource Exchange

Share and find notes, assignments and past papers for your courses

Welcome! This is a place for students to share and find study material. Upload your class notes, solved assignments, past papers and lab sheets, and browse what other students have shared for each course. Sharing a little helps everyone prepare better for quizzes and exams.

How it works

1. Pick a course

Choose your course from the list, or add a new course code if yours is missing.

2. Upload a file

Add a clear title, a short description and your file, then press Upload.

3. Browse and view

Use the tabs, search and filters to find material, then press View to read it.

Upload guidelines

  • Only share your own notes and work, or material your teacher has allowed you to share.
  • Give files a clear title, for example “MTH165 – Integration practice set”.
  • Choose the correct course and type so others can find it quickly.
  • Do not upload anything offensive or copied from a paid book.
  • Keep files under 25 MB.

Frequently asked questions

Which files can I view on the page?

PDFs, images and plain text or code files open directly in the viewer. Other files, such as Word documents, need to be downloaded first.

Where are my uploads stored?

They are saved in your own browser on this device, so other people cannot see them yet. A server would be needed for real sharing.

My course is not listed. What do I do?

Type the course code in the “Add course” box above the upload form. It will appear in the dropdown and the tabs.

Can I delete something I uploaded by mistake?

Yes. Press Delete next to the resource in the list.

Files are saved in this browser only, so they stay on your device.
